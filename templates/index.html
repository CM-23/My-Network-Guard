About My Network Guard

My Network Guard is a lightweight, high-performance Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS) designed to keep your home or office networks safe.

It passively monitors network traffic, builds hardware profiles, automatically flags new unknown devices, and uses advanced heuristics to detect DNS tunneling or out-of-hours activity.
