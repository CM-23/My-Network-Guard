## 2024-07-27 - Tuple evaluation over generator comprehension
**Learning:** Python's C implementation of `str.startswith()` and `str.endswith()` accepts tuples of strings and runs significantly faster than using a generator comprehension inside `any()`, particularly for frequent evaluations in threat detection loops. Furthermore, avoiding `Counter` for simple frequency maps and algebraic simplifications of entropy formulas (factoring out division and invariant log2 operations) reduces overhead per packet payload.
**Action:** Always prefer `str.startswith(tuple)` and `str.endswith(tuple)` over loop-based substring matching for sets of prefixes/suffixes. Use plain dict counting over `collections.Counter` and perform algebraic math simplifications in critical fast-path loops.
